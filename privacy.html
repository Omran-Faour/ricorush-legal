Privacy Policy

Updated: November 1, 2026
Developer: Auravion
App identifier: com.auravion.RicoRush

This policy explains how Auravion ("we", "us") handles information in RicoRush on Android and iOS and on this privacy-policy page.

Current iOS release: version 1.0

RicoRush 1.0 for iPhone and iPad is free and ad-free. It contains no advertising or advertising-consent SDKs and does not request tracking permission. The game does not collect or transmit personal information for advertising or gameplay analytics. Progress and preferences stay on your device. No account, sign-in or real-money purchase is required.

The planned advertising feature described below is not part of iOS 1.0. Any future iOS version that adds advertising will be a separate app update with revised privacy disclosures. Earlier iOS diagnostic builds with inactive advertising libraries also do not carry out the planned advertising processing.

1. Information used by the game

RicoRush does not require an account, sign-in, name, email address or other personal details to play. The game does not use a developer-operated cloud-save service or separate third-party gameplay analytics. Progress stays on your device. Information processing for a future ad-enabled version is described separately below.

The game stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Completed levels and unlocked levels.
  • Best scores and stars earned.
  • Sound and haptic-feedback preferences.
  • Earned in-game currency, unlocked cosmetics, equipped items and rescued Orbos.

This information is used to remember your progress and preferences between play sessions. The game does not request access to your contacts, precise location, photos, camera or microphone.

Future advertising (not included in iOS 1.0)

A future ad-enabled release is planned to use Unity LevelPlay mediation with ironSource Ads and Unity Ads to show occasional interstitial advertisements between levels. These services contact their servers when initialized and when loading or displaying an ad.

Advertising partners may collect and share IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device and operating-system details, SDK-generated user or installation identifiers, ad requests, impressions, clicks, and technical diagnostics. They use this information to deliver and measure ads, maintain their services and detect fraud. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing. RicoRush's shop uses earned in-game currency; the game does not report real-money purchase events to its advertising SDKs.

The planned ad-enabled configuration requests non-personalized advertising. Before initializing LevelPlay, it sends a negative GDPR consent signal, a sale/sharing opt-out signal and an advertising-device-ID opt-out signal. It does not supply a player name, email address, birth date or custom account identifier to the advertising SDK. The Android build removes advertising-ID permissions.

In a future ad-enabled iOS version, Apple's tracking-permission prompt may appear if no decision has been recorded. You may refuse without losing access to gameplay. An Apple permission decision does not grant consent to personalized advertising in RicoRush. You can review tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

For partner practices, international transfers, retention and privacy requests, see the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Unity ads provide a Data Privacy icon with available privacy choices. Those controls apply to Unity's advertising services, rather than every advertiser's destination website. We do not control partner retention periods, and deleting local progress does not delete data already held by advertising partners.

Contact us at the address below for assistance with privacy questions or requests. Clicking an advertisement may open another provider's app or website, which has its own privacy practices.

2. Diagnostics and app stores

The Android and iOS store release builds do not enable our development-only performance recorder or Unity cloud analytics and crash reporting. Separate diagnostic builds used during development can record device specifications, performance measurements and error logs locally; those recordings are not automatically uploaded by the game.

Google Play and Android operate separately from the game. Depending on your device settings and consent, Google may collect usage and diagnostic information and make crash or performance reports available to developers through Android vitals. We may use those reports to identify and fix technical problems. See Google's Privacy Policy and Android usage and diagnostics settings.

Apple and iOS operate separately from the game. Depending on your device settings and consent, Apple may collect diagnostic information and make crash or performance reports available to developers. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Android and iOS may back up or restore local app data according to your device and account settings. These backups are managed by your operating system or backup provider, not by a RicoRush cloud-save service.

3. When you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as your message, device details, screenshots or error reports. We use this information to respond and troubleshoot the issue. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Support messages are handled through our email provider. We do not sell or rent support correspondence or use it for advertising. Information may be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect legal rights.

4. Retention, deletion and security

To request deletion of information held by Auravion, email info@auravion.com with the subject "RicoRush privacy deletion". Describe the information you want deleted, such as a support conversation, and use the email address associated with it where possible. Do not send a password or identity document. We may ask for the minimum additional information needed to locate the record and verify the request.

Saved progress and preferences remain in the app's local storage until you clear them or remove the app, subject to your operating system's backup and restore behavior. To clear local data, open Android Settings, select Apps, then RicoRush, then Storage and choose Clear storage or Clear data. The exact labels depend on your device. On iOS, delete the app from Settings > General > iPhone Storage > RicoRush > Delete App; offloading alone retains app data. The in-game Reset Progress control also clears gameplay progress. These actions reset your progress. Manage any retained backups separately through your device or backup-provider settings.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations. A deletion request covers the support messages, attachments and contact details we hold about you, except information we must retain for legal obligations or resolving a dispute. We will explain any applicable retention exception when responding. There is no RicoRush account or developer-hosted gameplay profile to delete.

For information held by advertising partners, use the privacy controls in their ads or the request instructions in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Unity's Data Privacy icon can provide access and deletion choices for Unity Ads data. You can also contact Auravion for help identifying the appropriate partner request route. Clearing local game data does not itself delete partner records; partner retention and legal exceptions are explained in their privacy notices.

Local game data is stored using your operating system's application-storage facilities. We limit our handling of support information to responding to and resolving requests. No storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Intended audience

RicoRush is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not ask players to provide a birth date. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game or a support message, please contact us so we can investigate, assist with relevant partner requests and delete information we hold where appropriate.

6. This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or data-entry forms to this page. GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address and request logs, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our information practices change and revise the date above. Before releasing the advertising feature, we will review this policy and the store disclosures against the final build. If a future version changes features or data processing, the policy and any required in-app disclosures will be updated to describe those features.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Auravion at info@auravion.com.
