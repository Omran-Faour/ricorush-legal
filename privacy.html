RicoRush

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026
Developer: Auravion
App identifier: com.auravion.RicoRush

This policy explains how Auravion ("we", "us") handles information in RicoRush. It describes the current Android and iOS versions of the game and this privacy-policy page.

1. Information used by the game

RicoRush does not require an account, sign-in, name, email address or other personal details to play. The current versions covered by this policy do not display advertisements, use third-party gameplay analytics or provide a developer-operated cloud-save service. They do not automatically send your gameplay records to us.

The game stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Completed levels and unlocked levels.
  • Best scores and stars earned.
  • Sound and haptic-feedback preferences.
  • Earned in-game currency, unlocked cosmetics, equipped items and rescued Orbos.

This information is used to remember your progress and preferences between play sessions. The game does not request access to your contacts, precise location, photos, camera or microphone.

Advertising integration

The iOS 0.1.9 build 2 includes Unity LevelPlay and its Unity Ads adapter as preparation for a future advertising feature. Advertising is disabled in this build: the game does not initialize these advertising services or request ads, and it does not request permission to track you. Including an inactive advertising library is different from using it to collect advertising data.

Before enabling advertising in a future version, we will update this policy to describe the providers, information they process, purposes and available choices, and provide any required consent or parental-permission process. Children can play the current game without activating advertising. For information about Unity's services, see the Unity Privacy Policy.

Upcoming iOS privacy controls

We are preparing a consent-enabled iOS version. These controls are not included in iOS 0.1.9 build 2. In the upcoming version, players can choose under 18, 18 or older, or prefer not to say. The game stores only that age range on the device; it does not ask for a birth date or identity document. Players who choose under 18 or prefer not to say, or who have not answered, can keep playing without contacting the consent provider or activating advertising services.

For players who choose 18 or older, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks which privacy message applies and manages their choices. This requires contacting Google, including when advertising itself is switched off for testing. Google's iOS consent SDK declares processing of approximate location, performance information and product interactions for app functionality. Consent preferences are also stored on the device so the game and supported advertising services can respect them. We do not supply Google with a player account identifier, birth date or gameplay history. See Google's Privacy Policy.

In consent-enabled versions, open Settings > Privacy to access the policy, review Google's privacy choices where available, or turn ad services off on the device. Turning ad services off prevents further ad requests; restart the game to prevent previously started advertising services from initializing again. It does not erase Google's previously recorded choices. Gameplay remains available if consent is refused or the consent check fails. The upcoming consent setup does not itself turn advertising on or request permission to track you.

2. Diagnostics and app stores

The Android and iOS store release builds do not enable our development-only performance recorder or Unity cloud analytics and crash reporting. Separate diagnostic builds used during development can record device specifications, performance measurements and error logs locally; those recordings are not automatically uploaded by the game.

Google Play and Android operate separately from the game. Depending on your device settings and consent, Google may collect usage and diagnostic information and make crash or performance reports available to developers through Android vitals. We may use those reports to identify and fix technical problems. See Google's Privacy Policy and Android usage and diagnostics settings.

Apple and iOS operate separately from the game. Depending on your device settings and consent, Apple may collect diagnostic information and make crash or performance reports available to developers. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Android and iOS may back up or restore local app data according to your device and account settings. These backups are managed by your operating system or backup provider, not by a RicoRush cloud-save service.

3. When you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as your message, device details, screenshots or error reports. We use this information to respond and troubleshoot the issue. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Support messages are handled through our email provider. We do not sell or rent personal information or use it for advertising. Information may be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect legal rights.

4. Retention, deletion and security

Saved progress and preferences remain in the app's local storage until you clear them or remove the app, subject to your operating system's backup and restore behavior. To clear local data, open Android Settings, select Apps, then RicoRush, then Storage and choose Clear storage or Clear data. The exact labels depend on your device. On iOS, delete the app from Settings > General > iPhone Storage > RicoRush > Delete App; offloading alone retains app data. The in-game Reset Progress control also clears gameplay progress. These actions reset your progress. Manage any retained backups separately through your device or backup-provider settings.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us, subject to those obligations. There is no RicoRush account or developer-hosted gameplay profile to delete.

Local game data is stored using your operating system's application-storage facilities. We limit our handling of support information to responding to and resolving requests. No storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. Children

RicoRush does not ask players of any age to submit personal information while playing. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support message, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

6. This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or data-entry forms to this page. GitHub may process technical information, such as your IP address and request logs, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our information practices change and revise the effective date above. If a future version adds features such as online accounts, advertising or analytics, the policy and any required in-app disclosures will be updated to describe those features.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Auravion at info@auravion.com.
