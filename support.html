Support

Need help with RicoRush? Contact us with a question, bug report or feedback.

Email info@auravion.com

If the email button does not open your mail app, write to info@auravion.com directly.

Report a problem

To help us investigate, please include:

  • Your device model and Android or iOS version.
  • The RicoRush version you are using.
  • The level or screen where the issue occurred.
  • What happened, what you expected, and the steps to reproduce it.
  • A screenshot or short recording, if useful. Hide personal information before sending it.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information. A parent or guardian can contact us on a child's behalf.

Progress and troubleshooting

Try closing and reopening the game, and check your app store for updates. Avoid resetting progress, clearing app data or uninstalling while troubleshooting a save issue, as these actions may remove saved progress.

If your progress appears missing, email us with the details above before making further changes.

Feedback and content concerns

Suggestions and reports of inappropriate content are welcome at the same email address. If reporting an advertisement, include where and when it appeared and a screenshot if available. You do not need to click the advertisement.

Privacy questions

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent us, email info@auravion.com. Read our Privacy Policy for more information.
